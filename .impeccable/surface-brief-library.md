# Public boat library

Mode: Experience
Target: `/` (index) and `/boats/[slug]` (dossier)

## Audience and job

People interested in HydroStep or fast boats arrive curious. They look through existing boats and find a specific one by model. Success: they can recall that boat’s photograph and model name.

## Action and proof

Scan the Polaroid wall, use Find model, click a print. The wall shows only photograph and model name. The dossier holds extra fields and a photo library; dummy copy there is labeled dummy until real facts replace it.

## Constraints

Web. Astro with content collections. Owner edits records as files, not a public form. No prices, brokerage, or invented facts presented as real.

## Direction and moment

Workshop Polaroid Wall on open water. Memorable moment: one large print of the boat they care about, taped to live swell.

## Unresolved

Real photographs and real dossier facts still to replace dummy stand-ins.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The library is a Polaroid wall taped to open water — existing HydroStep hulls as instant prints you scan and find by model. It refuses the brokerage card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Full-bleed ocean swell as the wall; instant-film cream borders; masking tape; handwritten model names on the border; a rubber HYDROSTEP stamp; a paper “Find model” tab. Sea is dark blue-green with white wake; ink is carbon; photographs stay inside the film windows.

STORY: A curious visitor lands on the water, looks through a handful of HydroStep boats, finds one by model, and leaves able to recall its photo and model name.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Waves fill the entire frame. One large Polaroid sits at the center of gravity; six smaller prints orbit it. Stamp top-left. Find model is a paper tab, lower-left — not a website search bar. No navbar. Each print is photograph plus model only. Clicking opens `/boats/[slug]` as that print coming forward into a dossier.
